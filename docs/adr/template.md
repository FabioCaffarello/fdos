# ADR-NNNN — Short present-tense statement of the decision

## Context

What forces are at play? What makes this a decision rather than an obvious
choice? State the constraints honestly, including the ones that are
uncomfortable.

Reference the Constitution principle this decision serves or implements.

## Decision

State the decision in the present tense, actively. "FDOS adopts X." Not "we will
consider adopting X."

## Consequences

### Positive

What this buys, concretely.

### Negative

What this costs. An ADR with no negative consequences has not been thought
about; it has been advocated for. State the tax, the risk, and what would make
this decision wrong later.

### Enforcement

Which rung of the enforcement ladder (see docs/constitution.md §15) carries this
decision, and by what mechanism. If the answer is "human discipline", say so —
and say what would be needed to climb.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. An alternative dismissed
without a reason is not an alternative, it is a formality.

## Notes

Optional. Links, follow-up work, open questions this decision deliberately
leaves unresolved.
